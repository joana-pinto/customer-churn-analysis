# Customer Churn Analysis – Business Report

## Objective
The goal of this analysis is to identify the key factors driving customer churn and provide actionable recommendations to improve customer retention.

---

## Key Findings

- Customers on **month-to-month contracts** show the highest churn rate (~42%), significantly higher than long-term contracts.
- Customers with **less than 1 year of tenure** have the highest churn (~48%), compared to ~14% for customers with more than 2 years.
- Customers with **higher monthly charges** exhibit higher churn rates (~34%), suggesting pricing sensitivity.
- Long-term contracts (one-year and two-year) are strongly associated with customer retention.

---

## High-Risk Customer Segment

The customers most likely to churn are those who:
- Are **new (low tenure)**
- Have **month-to-month contracts**
- Pay **higher monthly charges**

This segment represents the most critical target for retention strategies.

---

## Business Implications

- **Customer lifecycle matters**: churn risk is highest in the early stages of the customer journey.
- **Contract structure is a key driver**: lack of commitment increases churn likelihood.
- **Pricing plays a role**, but is not the only factor influencing churn.

---

## Recommendations

- **Improve onboarding experience**  
  Focus on engaging customers during their first months to reduce early churn.

- **Promote long-term contracts**  
  Offer incentives (discounts, benefits) to encourage customers to switch from month-to-month plans.

- **Review pricing strategy**  
  Evaluate whether high monthly charges align with perceived customer value.

- **Target high-risk segments**  
  Use churn indicators (low tenure, high charges, short contracts) to identify and proactively retain customers.

---

## Next Steps

- Conduct **A/B testing** to evaluate retention strategies (e.g., pricing adjustments or contract incentives)
- Build a **predictive model** to identify customers at risk of churn
- Implement **customer segmentation** for more personalized retention actions
